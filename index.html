Square Harness
GitHub Documentation

Open-source research software

A harness for mathematical proof work.

Square Harness connects a language model to a simple workflow: solve, verify, and repair when needed. Every candidate and objection is saved for inspection.

Get started Explore the source

Solve

Give the model a substantial attempt at the whole problem.

Verify

Review the complete candidate and record concrete objections.

Repair

Revise when needed, keeping earlier candidates and reviews available.

Square Harness is experimental. A model's approval is not a mathematical certificate; independent checking remains necessary.
